隐私政策English

拾听让您在 App 内独立登录 Apple Music，浏览资料库、搜索音乐、安排队列，并整理本机收听记录。

使用条件

基础播放、登录、搜索、资料库和队列控制免费且无广告。新建队列书签、智能合集和保存歌单快照属于本地 Pro 工具，提供七天免费试用，之后可一次性买断。试用从确认免费交易起计算，不自动扣费或续费；重新安装和恢复购买不会重开试用。试用到期不停止音乐，已有记录的查看及原有导出功能保留。价格以购买页面显示为准。播放 Apple Music 完整歌曲需要另有有效订阅和网络连接；可用内容与功能取决于账号、地区及 Apple 服务。

登录与账号空间

在音乐账户页面点击登录，通过 Apple 官方授权窗口完成验证。设备的 iCloud 和“媒体与购买项目”账号保持原样。您可以建立和切换本机音乐账号空间；每个空间分别保存网页登录状态与收听记录。切换会停止原空间播放，新空间需自行登录和开始播放。退出登录可在当前空间的音乐账户页面操作。

播放与整理

支持资料库浏览、搜索、播放队列、随机与循环，以及本机书签、专辑标签、智能合集和歌单快照。快照只保存曲目列表，不含音乐文件。此版本不提供歌词或受保护音乐的离线下载。播放失败时，请先检查网络与 Apple Music 订阅，再到音乐账户页面检查登录状态。

反馈问题或建议

打开“设置 → 问题与建议”，或发送邮件至 329074990@qq.com。请提供操作步骤、实际与期望结果、App 和 iOS 版本。您可以先查看诊断，再决定是否附上；请勿发送密码、验证码或登录凭据。

购买与恢复

在设置中打开“购买与恢复”，可查看当地价格、确认免费试用、一次性买断或恢复已有购买。内购使用 App Store 账户，与 App 内登录的音乐账号分别管理。恢复购买可能要求 Apple 身份验证。购买 Pro 不包含 Apple Music 订阅，也不会修复音乐登录。

本机数据

收听工具中的清理功能管理本机记录，不会删除 Apple Music 云端歌单。诊断记录单独清理。详见 隐私政策。

Support

Rilivo provides an independently signed-in Apple Music session with library browsing, search, a playback queue and on-device listening tools.

Requirements

Basic playback, sign-in, search, library access and queue controls are free and ad-free. Creating queue bookmarks, smart collections and saving playlist snapshots are local Pro tools, with a seven-day free trial followed by an optional one-time purchase. The trial starts when you confirm the free transaction. There is no automatic charge or renewal; reinstalling or restoring purchases does not restart it. Expiry never stops music, and existing records and export options remain available. See the purchase screen for your local price. Full Apple Music playback requires a separate active subscription and an internet connection. Content and features depend on your account, region and Apple services.

Sign-in and music spaces

Choose Sign In on the Music Account page and complete Apple's official authorization. Your device's iCloud and Media & Purchases accounts remain unchanged. Each local music space has separate web sign-in data and listening records. Switching stops the previous space's playback; sign in and start playback yourself in the new space. Use the current space's Music Account page to sign out.

Playback and organization

Browse, search and manage your playback queue, shuffle and repeat. Save local bookmarks, album tags, smart collections and playlist snapshots. Snapshots contain track lists, not music files. This version does not offer lyrics or offline downloads of protected music. If playback fails, check your network, Apple Music subscription and sign-in status.

Contact

Open Settings → Problems & Suggestions or email 329074990@qq.com. Include steps, actual and expected results, and app and iOS versions. Review diagnostics before choosing whether to attach them. Never send passwords, verification codes or sign-in credentials.

Purchases and restoration

Open Purchases and Restore in Settings to see your local price, start the free trial, buy once or restore a purchase. Purchases use your App Store account, separately from the music account signed in inside Rilivo. Restoration may require Apple authentication. Pro does not include an Apple Music subscription or fix music sign-in.

Local records

Listening Tools manages local records without deleting Apple Music cloud playlists. Clear diagnostics separately. See the privacy policy.
