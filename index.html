支持与联系

隐私政策English support

拾听提供 Apple Music 浏览、播放、歌词和本机收听整理工具。使用音乐服务需要有效的 Apple Music 订阅，内容可用性取决于账户和曲库地区。

反馈问题或建议

在 App 中打开“设置 → 问题与建议”，或发送邮件至 329074990@qq.com。请描述操作步骤、实际结果和期望结果，并提供 App 与 iOS 版本。诊断附件由您自行决定；请勿发送密码、验证码或登录凭据。

登录与歌词

请在 App 内 Apple 官方页面完成登录。手机的 App Store 账户无需因此切换。歌词优先来自 Apple Music 当前网页会话；Apple 缺词时可查询 LRCLIB。备用查询默认开启，可在“设置 → 歌词”关闭。歌词页会显示来源，不能保证每首歌都有同步歌词、翻译或发音。

本机数据与隐私

收听工具中的清理功能管理本机收听记录，不会删除 Apple Music 云端歌单。诊断记录单独清理。详细数据处理和联系方式见 隐私政策。

Support

Rilivo provides Apple Music browsing, playback, lyrics and on-device listening tools. Music access requires an active Apple Music subscription; availability depends on the account and storefront.

Contact

Open Settings → Problems & Suggestions in the app, or email 329074990@qq.com. Include the steps, actual and expected results, and app and iOS versions. Diagnostic attachments are optional. Never send passwords, verification codes or sign-in credentials.

Sign-in and lyrics

Sign in through Apple’s official page inside the app without changing the device’s App Store account. Lyrics are requested from the current Apple Music web session first; when unavailable, LRCLIB can provide backup lyrics. Backup lookup is on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Lyrics. The source is shown on the lyrics page. Synced lyrics, translations and pronunciation are not available for every song.

Data controls

Listening Tools manages local records without deleting Apple Music cloud playlists. Diagnostics can be cleared separately. See the privacy policy for details.
