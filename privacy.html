Apple Music 登录与播放

拾听使用 Apple 官方 MusicKit JS。登录在 Apple 官方授权窗口完成，授权状态由此 App 中对应音乐空间的 WebKit 网页数据区保存。原生代码不读取或导出密码、Cookie、音乐用户令牌或受保护音频地址，也不更改设备系统账号。搜索、资料库、播放及封面请求由 Apple 服务处理；您主动进行的资料库操作会修改相应 Apple Music 数据。Apple 的处理方式见 Apple 隐私政策。

App Store 购买

下载购买由 App Store 处理。拾听不接收支付卡信息。本地工具无需应用内交易。

拾听音乐连接服务

App 会连接部署在腾讯云上的 rilivo.songhu.vip，以加载音乐连接页面和开发者授权配置。连接需要处理网络地址与请求信息。此服务不接收您的 Apple 密码、音乐用户令牌、资料库或本机收听记录；音乐授权和音乐请求交由 Apple 处理。

该站点已关闭常规访问日志。发生网络或服务错误时，服务器错误日志可能保留 IP 地址、请求路径、发生时间和技术错误信息，用于安全及故障处理，不用于广告或用户画像。错误日志按日检查轮换，保留最多 14 个轮换文件；空日志不轮换，不能把这一配置理解为所有记录固定保存 14 天。基础设施服务的信息处理还受 腾讯云隐私保护声明及相关服务条款约束。

音乐账号空间与本机记录

各空间分别保存网页登录数据、资料库缓存、播放历史、队列与进度、书签、屏蔽、标签、筛选及歌单快照。空间名称只在本机用于识别。部分通用设置和反馈草稿在 App 内共用。切换会停止原空间播放，不会自动为新空间登录或播放。

这些整理记录不会自动上传给开发者。系统备份可能包含部分 App 数据；导出文件由您选择的接收方和存储位置管理。您可以在收听工具中管理本机记录，在诊断页单独清理诊断。清理本机收听记录不会删除云端歌单、已导出文件或网页登录数据。退出 Apple Music 请使用对应空间的音乐账户页面。

诊断与支持邮件

App 的播放诊断只在本机保存播放事件、控制结果、时间和 App 状态，不自动上传，并排除在系统备份之外。反馈时由您预览并主动发送邮件，可自行选择是否附带诊断。邮件包含您填写的内容、App 与系统环境信息以及所选附件，发件地址和显示姓名由邮件服务提供。请勿发送登录凭据。

开发者通过 QQ 邮箱接收支持邮件，只将其中的信息用于处理问题、回复和落实隐私请求，并在支持所需期间保留，不再需要时删除。您可通过上述邮箱提出访问、更正或删除支持邮件的请求。邮件服务商依据其政策处理邮件；开发者无法替您删除 Apple 持有的数据。

Privacy policy

For version 0.64 · Revised November 7, 2026

Rilivo is provided by independent developer Song Hu. Contact 329074990@qq.com for privacy questions or data requests. One App Store purchase includes all local tools. Rilivo uses no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK.

Apple Music

Rilivo uses Apple's official MusicKit JS. Sign-in occurs in Apple's official authorization window. Each music space keeps its web authorization state in its own WebKit data store. Native code does not read or export passwords, cookies, music user tokens or protected audio URLs, and does not change system accounts. Apple handles search, library, playback and artwork requests. Library actions you initiate change the relevant Apple Music data. See Apple's privacy policy.

App Store purchase

The App Store handles the paid download. Rilivo does not receive payment card details. All local tools are included.

Rilivo's music connection service

The app connects to rilivo.songhu.vip, hosted on Tencent Cloud, to load its music connection page and developer authorization configuration. Network addresses and request information are processed to serve the connection. This service does not receive your Apple password, music user token, library or local listening records. Apple handles music authorization and music requests.

Routine access logging is disabled for this site. Server error logs may retain IP addresses, request paths, timestamps and technical errors for security and troubleshooting, without advertising or profiling. Rotation is checked daily and keeps up to 14 rotated files. Empty logs are not rotated, so this does not imply a fixed 14-day retention period for every entry. Infrastructure processing is also subject to Tencent Cloud's privacy statement and applicable service terms.

Music spaces and local records

Spaces keep separate web sign-in data, library caches, playback history, queue and progress, bookmarks, blocked items, tags, filters and playlist snapshots. Space names are local labels. Some general settings and feedback drafts are shared in the app. Switching stops the previous space's playback and does not sign in or start playback automatically in the new space.

Organization records are not automatically sent to the developer. System backups may include some app data. You choose where exported files go. Manage local records in Listening Tools and clear diagnostics separately. Clearing listening records does not delete cloud playlists, exports or web sign-in data. Sign out through the relevant space's Music Account page.

Diagnostics and support

Playback diagnostics stay on-device, recording events, control results, timing and app state. They are not uploaded automatically and are excluded from system backups. You review and send support email yourself, choosing whether to attach diagnostics. It includes your text, app and system context and selected attachments; your email provider supplies your sender address and display name. Do not send sign-in credentials.

The developer receives support through QQ Mail and uses the information only to address your issue, reply and handle privacy requests, retaining it while needed for support and deleting it when no longer needed. Contact the address above to request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence. Mail providers apply their own policies. The developer cannot delete data held by Apple for you.

Support website and updates

GitHub Pages hosts this support website and records visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement. This version does not provide lyrics or contact lyric services. We will update this page when features or data practices change.
