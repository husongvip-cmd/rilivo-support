Apple Music 登录与播放

App 在 Apple 官方网页中完成 Apple Music 登录与播放。登录状态保存在此 App 的 WebKit 网页数据区；App 的原生代码不读取或导出密码、Cookie 或登录令牌，不更改设备的 iCloud 或“媒体与购买项目”账号。搜索、目录、资料库、播放、封面及 Apple 歌词请求连接 Apple 服务。您主动执行的资料库和歌单操作会修改相应 Apple Music 数据。Apple 服务的数据处理适用 Apple 隐私政策。

Apple 歌词与 LRCLIB 备用歌词

App 优先读取当前 Apple Music 网页会话提供的歌词，在 App、已启用的歌词卡片或系统播放界面显示。需要歌词且 Apple 未提供歌词时，App 可查询 LRCLIB。备用查询默认开启；您可在“设置 → 歌词 → 备用歌词”中关闭，之前保存的关闭选择会保留。

备用查询发送当前歌曲的歌名、歌手、专辑和时长。必要时，App 向 Apple 公开目录发送数字歌曲 ID 和当前曲库地区以核对歌曲别名。LRCLIB 请求不含 Apple 登录信息或整份资料库；请求使用无 Cookie、无凭据存储及无持久 URL 缓存的临时会话。服务方仍会收到 IP 地址、客户端标识等连接信息。其日志、保留期限和处理地点由相应服务方决定。关闭备用查询会取消查询并移除当前备用歌词，不停止音乐播放；歌词正文不会写入播放诊断记录。

保存在设备上的数据

App 在本机保存偏好、浏览缓存、播放历史、队列与进度、收听观察、书签、屏蔽、标签、筛选及歌单快照，以支持浏览、整理与继续收听。反馈草稿也保存在本机。这些记录不会自动发送给开发者。系统备份可能包含部分 App 数据；您导出的文件由您选择的接收方和存储位置管理。

您可在收听工具中管理或清除本机记录，在诊断页单独清除诊断。清除本机收听记录不会删除 Apple Music 云端歌单、已导出的文件或网页登录数据。Apple 登录退出请使用 App 内 Apple 官方账户页面；App 卸载和系统备份的处理受操作系统控制。

诊断与反馈邮件

播放诊断在本机记录事件、控制结果、时间及 App 状态，不自动上传，不包含歌词正文，并排除在系统备份之外。通过“问题与建议”反馈时，由您预览并主动发送邮件，可自行选择是否附上诊断。邮件会包含您填写的内容、App 和系统环境信息，以及您选择附带的诊断；发件邮箱由您的邮件服务提供。请勿发送密码、验证码或其他账户凭据。

开发者使用 QQ 邮箱接收支持邮件，仅为处理您的问题、回复或落实隐私请求使用其中的信息。邮件和必要的处理记录在支持所需期间保存，不再需要时删除；邮件服务商按其政策处理邮件。您可向上述邮箱提出访问、更正或删除支持邮件的请求。开发者无法替您删除 Apple 或 LRCLIB 持有的数据。

广告、统计与网站访问

本 App 没有集成开发者的广告、行为统计或自动上传崩溃报告的 SDK。支持网站为静态页面，没有账号、表单或分析脚本。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管；访问时托管方会处理提供网站所需的网络信息，详见 GitHub 隐私声明。音乐及邮件服务可能在不同国家或地区处理数据，其处理地点由各服务方决定。

Privacy policy

Rilivo is provided by independent developer Song Hu. Contact 329074990@qq.com for privacy questions or requests.

Apple Music

Sign-in and playback take place in Apple’s official website inside the app. The web session is stored in the app’s WebKit data store. Native app code does not read or export passwords, cookies or sign-in tokens, and does not change the device’s iCloud or Media & Purchases account. Searches, catalog and library access, playback, artwork and Apple lyrics connect to Apple services. Library or playlist actions you initiate change the relevant Apple Music data. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Lyrics and LRCLIB

Rilivo first requests lyrics through the current Apple Music web session for display in the app and enabled lyric cards or system playback surfaces. When lyrics are needed and Apple has none, the app can query LRCLIB. Backup lookup is on by default. You can turn it off under Settings → Lyrics → Backup Lyrics; a previously saved off preference is preserved.

Requests send the current track’s title, artist, album and duration. When needed, an Apple public-catalog lookup uses the numeric song ID and actual storefront to check alternate track names. LRCLIB requests do not include your Apple sign-in information or full library. They use an ephemeral session without cookies, credential storage or a persistent URL cache. Providers still receive network information such as your IP address and client identification. Their logging, retention and processing locations are controlled by those providers. Turning backup lookup off cancels requests and removes the current backup lyrics without stopping music. Lyrics text is not written to playback diagnostics.

On-device records

Preferences, browsing cache, playback history, queue and progress, listening observations, bookmarks, blocked items, tags, filters, playlist snapshots and feedback drafts are stored on your device. Rilivo does not automatically send these records to the developer. System backups may include some app data. Files you export are controlled by the destination you choose.

You can manage or clear local listening records in Listening Tools, and clear diagnostics separately. Clearing local listening records does not delete Apple Music cloud playlists, previously exported files or web sign-in data. Use Apple’s account page inside the app to sign out. App removal and backups are managed by the operating system.

Diagnostics and support email

Playback diagnostics stay on-device and record events, control results, timing and app state. They are not uploaded automatically, do not contain lyrics text, and are excluded from system backups. If you contact support, you review and send the email yourself and choose whether to include diagnostics. The message contains your text, app and system context, and the diagnostic information you select; your email provider supplies your sender address. Do not send passwords, verification codes or account credentials.

The developer receives support mail through QQ Mail and uses it only to address your request, reply or handle a privacy request. Messages and necessary handling records are retained while needed for support and deleted when no longer needed. Email providers process messages under their own policies. Contact the address above to request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence. The developer cannot delete data held by Apple or LRCLIB on your behalf.

Advertising, analytics and this website

The app does not include the developer’s advertising, behavioral analytics or automatic crash-upload SDKs. This support website consists of static pages without accounts, forms or analytics scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; the host processes network information needed to serve the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Music and email services may process data in different countries or regions, as determined by each provider.
